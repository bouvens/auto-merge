Use an internal `STATUS_EMOJI: Record<ProbeStatus, string> = { ok: '✅', warn: '⚠️', error: '❌', 'n/a': '➖' }` — per CLAUDE.md "prefer objects for mappings instead of switch-case".    
    ```
    # Diagnose: {owner}/{repo}

    _checked at {checked_at}_  
    **Overall:** {ok ? '✅ ok' : '❌ issues found'}

    ## App
    - {emoji} installed: {detail or "yes (installation_id=N)"}
    - {emoji} permissions: actual={k:v,…}; required={k:v,…}; missing=[…]

    ## Config
    - {emoji} source: {source ?? 'none'}
    - main_branch: {…}
    - release_branch: {…}
    - dev_branch: {…}
    - errors: {joined or '-'}

    ## Branches
    - {branch_name}: exists={true|false}, protected={true|false}

    ## Notify
    - slack: {emoji} {status}
    - telegram: {emoji} {status}

    ## Onboarding
    - {emoji} {hint}
    - config_present: {true|false}
    - open_pr: #{N} {url}   (when present)
    ```
